# Security Policy

## Supported scope

Security reports are accepted for the current public release of this repository,
including the methodology website bundle and reusable templates. This repository
does not operate the hosted getgaldur.com service; reports about that service may use
the same private contact and will be routed to its operator.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Do **not** open a public issue or Discussion for a suspected vulnerability. Email
**galdur@yggnet.cz** with the affected file, URL, release, or commit; reproduction
steps and expected impact; and any proof of concept with secrets and personal data
removed.

We aim to acknowledge a report within **three business days** and provide an initial
assessment within **seven business days**. Resolution time depends on severity and
complexity; these targets are response goals, not a contractual SLA.

Avoid privacy violations, service disruption, social engineering, destructive testing,
and access to data that is not yours. Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate
before public disclosure.

## Public disclosure

Confirmed issues are coordinated with the reporter. A security advisory, fixed release,
or changelog entry will be published when appropriate. Never include credentials,
internal infrastructure details, customer data, or private correspondence in a report.
