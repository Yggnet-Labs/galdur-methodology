# Code of Conduct

GALDUR welcomes evidence-based discussion from people with different backgrounds,
roles, and levels of experience. Participation in issues, pull requests, Discussions,
and other project spaces requires professional and respectful conduct.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal qualities.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, attribution, and intellectual property.
- Make space for questions and correct mistakes openly.
- Follow maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, spam, and publishing private or security-sensitive information are
not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens people or the project.
Report conduct concerns privately to **galdur@yggnet.cz**. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical, with attention to conflicts of interest and safety.

This policy applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents the
project elsewhere.
