# Contributing to GALDUR

Thanks for your interest in GALDUR — a methodology for hybrid teams of people and AI agents.

This repository holds the **public, community-facing** part of GALDUR: the landing page,
the methodology (CZ + EN), the reusable templates, and community/legal pages.

## Ways to contribute

- **Report a bug** in the methodology or a template — use the *Methodology or template bug*
  issue template.
- **Request missing content** — a template, an example, or a methodology section that
  should exist — use the *Missing template or missing content* template.
- **Discuss** — share how you apply GALDUR in practice, ask questions, or propose ideas in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/Yggnet-Labs/Galdur-Methodology/discussions).
- **Use & adapt the templates** — the files in `templates/` are MIT-licensed. Fork them,
  adapt them to your team, and share back what works.

## Ground rules

- Keep it concrete and practical — GALDUR is about real delivery with acting AI agents.
- **Never** include secrets, credentials, internal infrastructure details, or private data
  in issues, discussions, or pull requests.
- Be respectful. We assume good intent and value clear, evidence-based feedback.

## Credit

Substantive contributions to the methodology are credited in the next release.
